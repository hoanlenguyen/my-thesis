The include and unsure counts come from a first pass with an AI assistant (Claude) on titles, with abstracts where a title was unclear. The author checks every decision, and a supervisor checks a random sample.

Review questions

  • LQ1, interpreting household plans (SQ1). How do language models turn what a household says into the inputs of a smart home energy management system, or schedule its loads themselves, and which checks, clarifications or fallbacks make their outputs reliable?
  • LQ2, configuring an optimiser (SQ2). How are language models used to choose the settings of an optimiser or controller, such as MPC, in home or building energy management, and against which baselines?
  • LQ3, on local hardware (SQ3). Which language models run on local or edge hardware for energy management or smart-home tasks, and how do they compare with cloud models in accuracy, latency, energy, cost and privacy?

Each review question mirrors one sub-question of the thesis (since 12 November 2026). For every study the review also records its evaluation (baselines, metrics, data). In the screening notes below, written before that date, "LQ2" marks a study about evaluation.

Search A answers LQ1 and LQ2 (language models of any size and home or building energy). Search B answers LQ3 (small or locally run language models and energy management in general).
