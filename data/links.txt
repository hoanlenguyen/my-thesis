For the literature review, here are some tentative search string examples for the main databases that you could try and modify. Please adapt them as needed:

Scopus (Advanced search):
TITLE-ABS-KEY ( ( "large language model*" OR llm* OR "agentic AI" OR "language agent*" ) AND ( "home energy management" OR "demand response" OR "smart home*" OR ( ( appliance* OR load* ) W/3 schedul* ) ) ) AND PUBYEAR > 2019 AND ( LIMIT-TO ( DOCTYPE , "ar" ) OR LIMIT-TO ( DOCTYPE , "cp" ) ) AND ( LIMIT-TO ( LANGUAGE , "English" ) )

Web of Science Core Collection (Advanced Search, then set the years with Refine):
TS=(("large language model*" OR LLM* OR "agentic AI" OR "language agent*") AND ("home energy management" OR "demand response" OR "smart home*" OR ((appliance* OR load*) NEAR/3 schedul*)))

IEEE Xplore (Command Search):
("All Metadata":"large language model*" OR "All Metadata":LLM* OR "All Metadata":"agentic AI" OR "All Metadata":"language agent*") AND ("All Metadata":"home energy management" OR "All Metadata":"demand response" OR "All Metadata":"appliance scheduling" OR "All Metadata":"smart home*")

ScienceDirect (field Title, abstract, keywords):
("large language model" OR LLM OR "agentic AI" OR "language agent") AND ("home energy management" OR "demand response" OR "appliance scheduling" OR "smart home")

Google Scholar (only for snowballing and grey literature):
"large language model" "home energy management" OR "demand response" OR "appliance scheduling" OR "load scheduling"

A few tips:
1. Keep a search log with the date, database, exact string, filters and number of results.
2. Export the results to RIS or BibTeX, remove duplicates in Zotero or Rayyan, and report the numbers in a PRISMA 2020 flow diagram.
