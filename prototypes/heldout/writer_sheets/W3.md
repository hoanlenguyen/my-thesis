# Writing messages to a home energy app

Thank you for helping. This takes about an hour.

## The home

Imagine you live in this house. It is a detached house in Konstanz, Germany, with:

- solar panels on the roof and a 10 kWh home battery;
- a heat pump that heats the house;
- an electric car with a 40 kWh battery, charged at home at 3.7 kW (about 10 % of the battery per hour);
- a dishwasher (a run takes about 1 hour) and a washing machine (a run takes about 2 hours 15 minutes).

A small computer in the house, the home energy gateway, plans when to charge the car, run the heat pump and the
appliances so that electricity is cheap. You tell it what you need through a chat in the home app, by typing or
speaking.

What the app already knows:

- the car's current charge (the app shows it with every message);
- the indoor temperature is kept between 20 and 22 degrees, unless you ask for something else;
- when everyone is out, the house may cool down to 17 degrees;
- the usual car charge: 80 % by 07:30 every day, set once in the app.

## What to do

Each situation below gives a date and time, the car's charge and what you need. Write the message you would
send the app at that moment. (The dates are in 2016 because the house's measured data are from that year; write
as if it were today.)

- **Use your own words.** Write it the way you would really type or say it to the app, as short or as long as you
  like. Please don't copy the sentences of the situation.
- **Write the times your way** ("half seven", "by 6pm", "tomorrow morning at 7"), as long as the meaning is the
  one in the situation.
- **Some situations ask you to keep one thing vague.** Do that, and give everything else as usual.
- **Some situations ask for something that cannot work.** Ask for it anyway, as the situation says, without
  pointing out the problem.
- **Some situations have a later second message**, sent when the plan changes. Write it as you would send it at
  that later time.
- Write in English. Spelling and grammar do not matter.

Please don't show your messages to the other writers, and don't ask how the app works or what it will answer:
the point is to see how it copes with messages written by different people.

Return the filled-in sheet to the researcher. Your messages are used only for this thesis and are stored without
your name.
